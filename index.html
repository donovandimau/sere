From a farewell shared across the ocean to a familiar melody far from home. Sere is a place to discover Fiji through its music.

Our music & sources

“Available to play” means Sere has an original or documented licensed recording. “Rights pending” means you can explore the song’s credits and reference, while lyrics, translations and recordings stay out of this preview until rights evidence is recorded. A source link is attribution, not a licence. The supplied catalogue is preserved in the repository.
